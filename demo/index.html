Your AI writes the note. In the voice you pick.

Turn the note on and the card hands Home Assistant's AI the hours ahead: when the rain starts and stops, the high and the low, the gusts. It writes back a line and a longer note, and the card shows whichever fits. Every screen you sign in on shares that one note.

Your station sets the numbers. The forecast still paints the sky.

Point the card at your own sensors and it shows what they measure: temperature, feels like, humidity, wind and pressure, indoors and out. Your wind also sets how fast the weather moves; the sky still comes from your weather entity.

Tune the weather. More rain, bigger clouds, a higher splash.

Seven settings scale the engine. Take hold of one and the card shows the weather it changes; let go, and the new weather settles in. The editor's sliders cover the useful range, and YAML takes more.

Paint your own sky. A colour per condition, and the sun, rain and snow to match.

Each sky colour becomes a whole sky for that condition, lighter overhead and deeper at the horizon, with the same shading behind the metrics, at every hour. Start from a palette, or set each colour yourself. Sky palette only.

colors.yaml

The day, drawn by the sun. Now, and the next sunrise or sunset.

The sun tracker comes in the same download. It draws the sun's path through your day, counts down to the next sunrise or sunset, and, with Home Assistant's Moon integration, names the moon's phase.

Needs an integration added

Planes
The Flightradar24 integration, a custom integration you install from HACS.
Moon phase
Home Assistant's Moon integration. Add it in Settings, under Devices and services.
Weather note
An AI integration with an AI Task, such as OpenAI or Anthropic, on Home Assistant 2025.8 or later. The notes on this page are samples, written by hand.
